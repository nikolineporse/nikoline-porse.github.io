About

Nikoline has spent her career getting brands talked about — from luxury retail floors to five-star product launches to the pages of Financial Times. Los Angeles-born and New York-made, she moved east about a year after graduating from the University of Oregon, and hasn't looked back.

She got her start on the sales floor at Aviator Nation, her first full-time gig in the city, while juggling two very different side hustles: a short-lived fling as a personal assistant, and an internship at CLD PR that turned into a crash course in agency life. There, she built press lists and drafted pitches in Cision and MuckRack, ran event activations and celebrity seeding campaigns for brands like Celsius (including Coachella's Cosmic Desert Party), and coordinated NYFW gifting suites.

From there, she joined Assouline's client team, where she's helped bring premier product launches to life — Roger Federer's book signing, Derrick Rose's Rose's Flower Shop Valentine's Day event, the Carbone book launch, The Mark Hotel book launch, and Alain Elkann's Q&A and second-volume release among them — while managing a high-net-worth, celebrity clientele through Endear.

Today, she's also shaping content strategy at Stacy McLaughlin Communications. She started as an Account Coordinator landing placements in Financial Times and Interior Design Magazine, and now runs point as Social Media Manager, producing content for Stacy's PR Instagram to spotlight client placements and happenings, offering social media guidance to clients, and occasionally shooting content for them to use.

A Public Relations graduate of the University of Oregon (minors in Philosophy and Sociology), Nikoline is still chasing what pulled her into this industry in the first place: brands with a point of view, and a story worth telling loudly.
