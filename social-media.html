The Approach

The goal is not to post for the sake of posting. It is to find the strongest angle, choose the format that serves it and make every piece feel connected to the larger story.

  1. 01

    Find the Story

    Identify the press win, project detail, event moment or client news worth elevating.

  2. 02

    Shape It for the Platform

    Select the right format and edit the visual and written story for how people use Instagram.

  3. 03

    Credit the Collaboration

    Coordinate names, tags and attribution across clients, creatives, publications and partners.

  4. 04

    Build Continuity

    Maintain a recognizable voice and visual rhythm so every post contributes to a stronger whole.
